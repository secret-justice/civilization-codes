Traces of Ink — Recovering the Time Inside a Brushstroke

书法最难的部分不是字形，而是笔法——一个只在时间里存在的动作；而我们今天看到的书法，全都是静止的结果。本作品用实时笔迹采样把笔法还原成可测量、可比较、可重演、可聆听的数据：你写一笔，系统当场算出速度曲线、提按节奏、转折角与驻笔时长，指出“这一笔更接近颜体的宽厚还是赵体的圆润”，并把笔法映射成程序合成的声音。 What is hardest in Chinese calligraphy is not the character but the brushwork — an action that exists only in time; and what we see today is always the still result. This work uses real-time ink sampling to recover brushwork as data that can be measured, compared, replayed and heard: write one stroke, and the system computes its velocity curve, press rhythm, turning angles and dwell time, tells you whether it is closer to Yan’s breadth or Zhao’s roundness, and maps the brushwork into synthesised sound.

01 · 书写台Writing pad

写一笔，让笔法显影Write one stroke; let the brushwork develop

宣纸质感由代码生成，不是贴图。笔迹的粗细与飞白随速度变化：快则细而见枯笔，慢则粗而墨浓；若使用压感笔，压力也参与笔道宽度，鼠标没有压感时则以速度为提按的代理。写完一笔，右侧立刻给出十维特征。 The paper grain is generated in code, not an image. Stroke weight and “flying white” follow velocity: fast thins the line and dries the hairs, slow thickens it; with a pressure pen, pressure joins in, and a mouse without pressure uses velocity as the proxy for press. One finished stroke and the ten features appear on the right.

这是一块可书写的画布。等价的文字说明：在画布上按住并拖动即可留下笔迹；系统随后在右侧列出这一笔的速度均值、速度方差、提按比、转折角、驻笔时长等十维特征，并以折线图与剖面图呈现。速度均值按 1000 像素参考画布折算；提按比在有真实压感时取自笔压，设备不提供压力时（鼠标压力恒为 0.5）改以行笔速度为代理，慢则按、快则提。若无法使用指针设备，请打开下方的“键盘书写”开关，用方向键移动笔尖、空格落笔与收笔。

书写时墨量与飞白由速度实时决定；键盘模式下方向键移动、空格落笔、Esc 取消这一笔。 Ink load and flying white follow your speed live. In keyboard mode: arrow keys move, space lifts or lowers the tip, Esc cancels the stroke.

02 · 笔法解构Brushwork analysis

十维笔法特征 · 实时Ten brushwork features · live

速度均值按 1000 px 参考画布折算（与八书体档案同量纲，悬停看屏幕实测）；提按比有压感用笔压，无压感以速度为代理。 Mean speed is normalised to a 1000 px reference canvas (hover for the raw value); press ratio uses pen pressure when reported, otherwise travel speed.

转折角标注Turn angles

把鼠标移到速度曲线上，画布上会高亮对应的笔迹位置；拖动“时间轴回放”可重看书写过程。 Hover the velocity curve to highlight the matching point on the canvas; drag the timeline to replay the stroke.

03 · 名家比对Style matching

你的笔法更接近谁Whose brushwork is yours closer to

用十维特征与八件书体档案（欧 / 颜 / 柳 / 赵 / 王羲之行书 + 隶书三家：曹全碑 / 张迁碑 / 石门颂）做加权余弦相似度计算，给出排序与解释。这是教学示意性的特征相似度，不构成书家、年代或真伪鉴定——所以我们只说“更接近”。 Ten features are compared with five style profiles (Ou, Yan, Liu, Zhao, Wang’s running script) by weighted cosine similarity, giving a ranking and an explanation. This is pedagogical feature similarity, not an attribution or a dating — hence “closer to”, never “is”.

    方法：十维特征按各维量纲归一化（角度类按笔迹轴向折叠）后加权余弦；权重公开于 data_steles.js 的 FEATURE_META。措辞统一为“更接近”。 Method: features normalised per dimension (angles folded to stroke axis), then weighted cosine; weights are public in FEATURE_META of data_steles.js. The wording is always “closer to”.

    04 · 笔法重演Brushwork replay

    把书体的笔法重新走一遍Walk the brushwork of a style again

    这不是字帖贴图，也不是预录动画：从永字八法的归一化骨架点列出发，按所选书体的结构参数（粗细、提按幅度、转折柔度、收笔外扩、中宫、欹侧、波势）重建轨迹与粗细，再由速度包络反解每一帧的时刻，得到带时间戳的重演序列。可重演的书体就是书体档案里的全部 8 种（欧 / 颜 / 柳 / 赵 / 王羲之行书 + 隶书三家：曹全碑 / 张迁碑 / 石门颂）；碑帖廊的 8 件碑帖因此全部在重演之列。 Not a bitmap, not a recorded animation: starting from the normalised skeleton of the Eight Principles of 永, the trajectory and weight are rebuilt from the style’s structural parameters (weight, press range, turn softness, exit flare, centre, slant, wave), then a velocity envelope is inverted into a timestamp for every frame. Replayable styles are exactly the five in the archive (Ou, Yan, Liu, Zhao, Wang’s running script); the three clerical steles in the gallery have no profile yet and are not replayable.

    05 · 听笔法Hear the brushwork

    不认得汉字，也能听出书体的性格——现在可以在作品里直接 A/B 对比听出来Without reading a single character you can hear a style’s temperament — and now you can A/B it directly inside the work

    “不认得汉字，也能听出书体的性格”这句话，现在可以在作品里直接 A/B 对比听出来：下面把两档书体档案并排发声，先听 A、再听 B。全部声音由 Web Audio 实时合成，不加载任何音频文件：速度→音高（五声音阶离散化），提按→音量包络，转折→谐波亮度。同一笔在同一音色下必然产生同一串音——种子来自量化后的十维特征，不含任何随机状态。 “You can hear a style without reading a character” is now verifiable inside the work as a direct A/B: two profiles sound side by side — listen to A, then to B. All sound is synthesised live by Web Audio; not one audio file is loaded. Velocity → pitch (a pentatonic quantisation), press → amplitude envelope, turns → harmonic brightness. The same stroke in the same timbre always yields the same notes, seeded by the quantised ten features with no hidden randomness.

    建议戴耳机。若浏览器要求交互后才允许发声，点击“听这一笔”即可（首次点击会激活音频上下文）。 Headphones recommended. Browsers require a gesture before audio starts; the first click activates the audio context.

    八体 A/B 对照试听Eight-style A/B audition

    同一根线条，两种性格——先听 A，再听 B。 One line, two temperaments — listen to A, then to B.

    A 与 B 都不是录音：两者由同一个确定性 sonify() 管线、按各自书体档案的十维特征实时合成，音色相同、都压到 4 秒。所以听到的差别只来自两份档案——速度→音高走向、提按→音量包络、转折→谐波亮度。预置三对之外，也可以在上面两个下拉里任选 8 档中的任意两档。 Neither A nor B is a recording: both are synthesised live by the same deterministic sonify() pipeline from the ten features of each profile — same timbre, both fitted to four seconds. Every difference you hear therefore comes only from the two profiles: speed→pitch contour, press→loudness envelope, turns→harmonic brightness. Beyond the three presets, any two of the eight profiles can be paired in the selects above.

    预置对照Presets

    连播用 A 的确定时长作定时器，输出仍然确定；点“停止声音”可随时中断。 The A→B chain is timed by A’s deterministic duration; “Stop” interrupts at any time.

    06 · 碑帖廊Stele gallery

    八件公版碑帖，同一“永”字的八种笔意Eight public-domain steles, eight hands on one character

    八件均为公有领域作品（东汉至元代）。卡片中的字形不是拓本，而是团队依各碑笔意自绘的矢量骨架——同一“永”字，按八件碑帖的结构参数（粗细、方折、波磔、中宫、欹侧）重新生成，因此8 碑的结构可以直接横向比较。可比对范围同样说清楚：8 件全部有对应书体档案（欧 / 颜 / 柳 / 赵 / 王羲之行书，加隶书三家曹全碑 · 秀逸 / 张迁碑 · 方整 / 石门颂 · 野逸），因此8 档档案也全部可参与名家比对、笔法重演与 A/B 试听，卡片上逐张标注“可比对 · 可重演”，没有一件是“仅静态展示”。点击图形切换“骨架线 / 字形轮廓”。 All eight are in the public domain (Eastern Han to Yuan). The glyphs are not rubbings but vector skeletons drawn by the team in each stele’s manner — the same 永 regenerated from eight sets of structural parameters, so the structure of all eight can be compared side by side. Scope, stated plainly: all eight have a matching style profile (Ou, Yan, Liu, Zhao, Wang’s running script, plus the three clerical hands Cao Quan · refined, Zhang Qian · square, Shimen · untrammelled), so all eight profiles take part in style matching, brushwork replay and the A/B audition; every card carries a “comparable · replayable” badge and none is “static display”. Click a figure to switch bone lines / outlines.

    书体档案：8 种（楷 / 行五档 + 隶书三家）· 收录碑帖：8 件 · 其中可比对 / 可重演 8 件（含隶书三家） Style profiles: 8 (five regular / running, three clerical) · steles: 8 · all 8 comparable / replayable

Hand it to a teacher, or compare it across three lessons.
